Pa reklama. Pa llogari. Tekstet, fjalët dhe aktivitetet punojnë edhe pa internet.

## English (secondary)

**Subtitle**
Albanian songs for kids 2–6

**Promotional text**
36 joyful Albanian songs for diaspora kids — lyrics, new words, activities, and finger‑drawing. Learn Albanian by singing!

**Keywords**
albanian,shqip,songs,kids,learn,language,diaspora,nursery,rhymes,preschool,toddler,education

**Description**
"Mëso shqip përmes këngëve" (Learn Albanian Through Songs) is a songbook for children
ages 2–6, made for Albanian diaspora families who want to keep the Albanian language alive.

Each song includes full lyrics, a video, new vocabulary, learning goals, and a hands‑on
activity with a finger‑drawing canvas. 36 songs in colorful cards, plus a Parent Guide.

No ads. No accounts. Lyrics, words, and activities work fully offline.
