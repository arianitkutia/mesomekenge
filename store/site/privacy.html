Overview

"Mëso shqip përmes këngëve" (the "App") is an offline songbook that helps children (ages 2–6) learn Albanian through songs, vocabulary, and activities. It is designed to be safe for children and collects no personal information.

Information we collect

The App itself collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Lyrics, vocabulary, activities, and the drawing canvas run entirely on the device and need no network connection.

Third-party video content (YouTube)

Songs can be watched as embedded YouTube videos, and song artwork uses YouTube thumbnail images. When a video or thumbnail loads, the request goes to Google/YouTube, and Google may collect data (such as IP address and device information) under its own policies. This happens only when video/thumbnail content is displayed.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for young children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not build profiles, serve targeted advertising, or share data with third parties for marketing.

Data we do not collect

No names, emails, contacts, location, photos, microphone/camera access, purchases, or usage analytics are collected or stored by the App.
