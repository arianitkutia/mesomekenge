## Overview
"Mëso shqip përmes këngëve" ("the App") is an offline songbook that helps children
(ages 2–6) learn Albanian through songs, vocabulary, and activities. We designed it to
be safe for children and to collect **no personal information**.

## Information we collect
**The App itself collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign‑in, no ads,
no analytics, and no tracking. Lyrics, vocabulary, activities, and the drawing canvas all
run entirely on the device and require no network connection.

## Third‑party video content (YouTube)
Songs can be watched as embedded **YouTube** videos, and song artwork uses YouTube
thumbnail images. When a video or thumbnail loads, the request goes to Google/YouTube,
and **Google may collect data** (such as IP address and device information) under its own
policies. This happens only when video/thumbnail content is displayed.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- YouTube Terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

We do not control and are not responsible for Google's data practices. If you prefer no
third‑party requests, use the App without playing videos (all other content is offline).

## Children's privacy
The App is intended for young children and does **not** knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not build profiles, serve
targeted advertising, or share data with third parties for marketing.

## Data we do not collect
No names, emails, contacts, location, photos, microphone/camera access, purchases, or
usage analytics are collected or stored by the App.
